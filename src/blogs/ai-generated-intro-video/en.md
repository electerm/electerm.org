# Electerm Intro Video — 100% AI Generated

We just published a new intro video for electerm: [Electerm Introduction Video](/videos/electerm-introduction-video/).

What makes this one special is not just the content — it is how it was made: **the whole video is AI generated**, from script to voiceover to editing, using `workbuddy AI` with the `deepseek v4.1flash` model. No camera, no microphone, no timeline scrubbing by hand.

Original on Bilibili: [electerm 介绍视频](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ufaq6gE61/).

## What is in the video

About 3 minutes total (199 seconds), in two parts:

- **P1 `electerm-intro-cn` (~98s)** — Chinese narration: what electerm is, which protocols it covers, and which platforms it runs on.
- **P2 `electerm-intro-en` (~101s)** — English version of the same tour.

The script covers the essentials:

- Electerm is a **free and open-source** terminal / SSH / SFTP / FTP / Telnet / Serial / RDP / VNC / Spice client.
- One app for **Linux, macOS, Windows, Android, HarmonyOS and iOS**.
- Plus long-tail support people often miss: Ubuntu 18, Windows 7, macOS 10+, domestic Linux distros like UOS / Kylin, LoongArch (old-world and new-world), RISC-V (`riscv64`) and PowerPC 64-bit little-endian (`ppc64le`) Linux.

If you want the full written tour, start with [Introducing Electerm](/blogs/electerm-introduction/).

## How it was made — all AI

The Bilibili description says it plainly:

> 完全 AI 生成的 electerm 介绍视频，使用 workbuddy AI (deepseek v4.1flash) 生成。

In practice that means:

1. Gave workbuddy AI the product facts (features, protocols, platform list).
2. It drafted the CN + EN scripts, generated the voiceovers, assembled the visuals, and rendered subtitles.
3. Human work was review duty: fact-check the platform list, fix pronunciation of terms like `LoongArch` / `riscv64` / `ppc64le`, and upload.

Total hands-on editing time: close to zero. Compare that with the classic screen-recorded tutorials like [Electerm Usage Demo](/videos/electerm-usage-demo/) — great for showing clicks, but every retake costs a recording session.

## Why an AI intro

- **Two languages for free.** Recording CN + EN with a human voice doubles the work; with AI it is just a second render.
- **Docs change, video can keep up.** When the platform list grows, regenerate the narration instead of re-recording.
- **Short and rewatchable.** At ~100 seconds per language, it fits the "send this to a friend who asks what electerm is" slot that a 10-minute demo cannot fill.

It does not replace the hands-on guides — if you want clicks and settings, the [video guides](/videos/) (40+ short demos, one per feature) are still the place to go. Think of this one as the trailer, the others as the manual.
